Test Bunny Bounce on Android

Do these three steps on your phone or tablet, in order. Use the same Google account for all three.

  1. Join the tester group

    Tap Join group at the top of the page.

    Join the group

  2. Become a tester

    Tap Become a tester. When it says You are a tester, come back here.

    Become a tester

  3. Install the game

    This opens Bunny Bounce: Garden Hop in the Play Store. Tap Install.

    Install Bunny Bounce

Google Play needs 12 testers for 14 days, so please keep it installed for two weeks. If Install isn't there yet, wait a few minutes after step 2 and tap the button again.

Tell us what you think, good or bad, at gothriveforge@gmail.com. We'll never ask for a password or a code.
